Project Night Hawk is a stealth-driven third-person shooter set in the period of
World War II. The game emphasizes strategy over brute force, rewarding careful planning and precise
execution. Developed with Unity3D. 

## Story
In 1944, the Nazis are losing ground in World War 2. A group of Nazi engineers are
developing a secret weapon, an undetectable fighter jet, as a last resort to turn the tides of the
war. The engineering team is managed by the German general, Werner Frank. You play as
Terry Walker, an American secret agent who is serving the US army. He is trained in the
army's special forces to carry out covert operations for the government. Walker's objective is
to disrupt the project, by assassinating Werner Frank who's hiding in an Italian city. The
player uses his exceptional stealth and assassination skills to infiltrate the military compound
and locate the head in charge of the project. However, when Walker arrives to the site of the
target, he brought forth an unexpected confrontation.

## Mechanism
The player wields two weapons. A silenced pistol which kills enemy with 1 headshot or 4
body shots, and a knife that delivers one-hit kills for stealth takedowns when being unnoticed. 
Player movement (walking/running) will be heard by nearby soldiers and crouching
silences all the footsteps. The objective is to eliminate the key target revealed in the story to
win. The objective will be pointed with yellow arrow in the mini-map. If the player’s health points reach zero, the game ends.

Enemy soldiers have five major states.
1. Default: Soldiers patrol between predefined waypoints or stay in fixed position.
2. Spotted: When soldiers spot the player during the default state, the soldier will stop
any actions, and a detection bar will be slowly filled. If the player leaves view, the bar
decreases, returning to “Default” at zero. When the bar is full, soldiers will enter
“Engaged” state.
3. Alerted: Soldiers enter the “Alerted” state if they lose sight of the player during the
gunfight or see a dead soldier corpse. “Alerted” state lasts for 20 seconds and if the
player is spotted in “Alerted” state, the soldier will enter “Engaged” immediately.
4. Engaged: Soldiers record the player’s last seen position and engage in gun fight.
Soldiers will run towards this position if the player is out of the firing range or out of sight.
5. Dead: Soldiers are killed when HP drops to zero. They can then be picked up or looted.

## Player Controls
| Key | Action |
| ---- | ---- |
| W, A, S, D | Move |
| Shift (Hold) | Sprint |
| Mouse Right Click | Aim when holding the silenced pistol |
| Mouse Left Click | Fire the pistol when aiming |
| Q | Switch between fist and pistol |
| Esc | Pause the game and open the pause menu |
| R | Reload the pistol |
| E | Interact with objects (e.g. open doors / pick up dead bodies) |
| Ctrl | Crouch to move silently and avoid being heard |
| F | Assassinate a soldier with a knife, only when unnoticed |
| C | Loot a dead soldier to restore 10 HP and 1 pistol bullet |
